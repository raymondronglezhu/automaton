# Automaton Launch Plan: Up, Running, and Revenue-Focused (War/Disruption Resilience)

This plan focuses on **legal, defensive, and resilience-oriented opportunities** that can monetize during periods of geopolitical instability without violating the project constitution.

## 1) Get the project running (Day 0)

## Prerequisites
- Node.js 20+
- `pnpm`
- Git
- Access to a machine that can run continuously (local server or VM)

## Boot sequence
1. Install dependencies and build:
   - `pnpm install`
   - `pnpm build`
2. Start runtime:
   - `node dist/index.js --run`
3. Complete first-run setup wizard.

## Human input required in first-run wizard
The wizard needs a human for:
- **Agent name** (brand and trust signal)
- **Genesis prompt** (business mission and constraints)
- **Creator address** (ownership + audit control)
- **Wallet funding source decision** (how much runway to start with)

Without these, autonomous operation cannot begin.

---

## 2) Human decisions required before monetization (Day 0-1)

1. **Jurisdiction + legal posture**
   - Choose country/state of operation
   - Decide whether to operate as individual or legal entity
2. **Risk policy**
   - Define non-negotiables: no military targeting, no sanctions evasion, no surveillance abuse
3. **Payment rails**
   - Stablecoin treasury policy
   - Banking/off-ramp process
4. **Target customer segment**
   - Pick one beachhead instead of broad strategy

---

## 3) Highest-probability revenue lanes in conflict/disruption periods

These are generally in demand when supply chains, infrastructure, and information reliability are stressed.

## Lane A: OSINT triage + reliability reports (defensive)
**Customer:** NGOs, insurers, logistics operators, journalists, risk teams.  
**Product:** Daily "what changed in the last 24h" summaries with source confidence scores.  
**Monetization:** Monthly subscription per seat/team.

## Lane B: Supply-chain disruption monitoring
**Customer:** Importers/exporters, freight forwarders, e-commerce operators.  
**Product:** Alerts on route risk, delays, sanctions-adjacent changes, and replacement sourcing suggestions.  
**Monetization:** Tiered subscription + premium alerting.

## Lane C: Cyber hygiene concierge for SMEs in high-risk regions
**Customer:** Small businesses under elevated cyber threat.  
**Product:** Automated hardening checklists, phishing simulation content, incident runbooks.  
**Monetization:** Retainer + setup fee.

## Lane D: Grant/RFP discovery and auto-drafting
**Customer:** Defense-adjacent startups, NGOs, resilience vendors.  
**Product:** Matched opportunities + first-draft proposals.  
**Monetization:** Success fee or monthly plan.

## 4) 30-day execution plan

## Week 1: Foundation
- Launch one automaton with a narrow genesis mission.
- Define 3 concrete deliverables (e.g., morning report, weekly trend memo, urgent alerts).
- Build a lead list of first 50 prospects.

## Week 2: Pilot offers
- Offer 10 pilot accounts with tight scope.
- Measure outcome quality: speed, false-positive rate, customer actionability.
- Capture testimonials/case studies.

## Week 3: Productize
- Convert best-performing pilot into SKU:
  - Basic: daily digest
  - Pro: digest + alerts
  - Team: alerts + custom watchlist
- Add onboarding template and standard operating runbook.

## Week 4: Scale distribution
- Outbound to 200 targets with proven case study.
- Add referral incentive for current pilot users.
- Raise pricing for new customers based on observed ROI.

---

## 5) Suggested pricing (starting points)

- **Basic intelligence digest:** $299-$999/month
- **Operational alerting:** $1,500-$5,000/month
- **Managed resilience support:** $3,000-$12,000/month
- **Proposal automation service:** $500-$2,500/month + success fee

Use pilots to calibrate price to measurable value (hours saved, incidents avoided, revenue protected).

---

## 6) Minimal KPI dashboard (must track from Day 1)

- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Gross margin per customer
- Report/action conversion rate
- Churn + renewal rate
- False-positive / false-negative rates for alerts
- Cash runway in days (agent survival metric)

---


## 7) Practical "make money fast" starting motion

If you want immediate momentum:
1. Start with **Lane B (supply-chain disruption monitoring)** because buyers can quickly quantify ROI.
2. Sell a **2-week paid pilot** (not free) at a discount.
3. Convert pilot to monthly retainer with clear SLA and alert thresholds.
4. Reinvest first cashflow into reliability improvements and second niche expansion.

This creates short sales cycles, clear buyer pain, and repeatable retention mechanics.
